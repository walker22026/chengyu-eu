Elmo is seriously good. This AI browser extension caught me off guard.

Whether you're on a Chinese site or an English one, it can summarize the page's content almost instantly, which is a real efficiency boost — knowing the gist of an article up front helps you decide whether it's worth reading in full.

It's also surprisingly useful for language learning: when you're just starting out, getting the gist first makes it much easier to get into the actual article afterwards.
