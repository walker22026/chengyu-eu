Tonight I tried out Google's newly released video-generation AI. Just like I guessed a few days ago, it's already been rolled down into the Pro plan.

Every AI-generated clip comes out at eight seconds long. I spent tonight really putting it through its paces.

<video controls src="https://media.chengyu.eu/images/2025/06/09/80dafe474ba3ae0a9ad45d0249fc54e4.mp4"></video>

The most obvious takeaway: eight seconds from the Pro tier is still too short. A lot of scenes don't even have time to set up properly before the clip ends. That said, what actually makes it into those eight seconds is still fairly rich.

<video controls src="https://media.chengyu.eu/images/2025/06/09/bad0b513901a047aa45b687a052a4df2.mp4"></video>

<video controls src="https://media.chengyu.eu/images/2025/06/09/download-1.mp4"></video>

But there's one clear problem: Chinese-language support isn't great. Chinese keywords I typed in would either get read as English or just dropped entirely.

<video controls src="https://media.chengyu.eu/images/2025/06/09/f58c4f89b5008f0c25d75ed04e5608a7.mp4"></video>

If I forced it to use Chinese text, I'd often end up with garbled characters. Sticking to English text worked fine.

<video controls src="https://media.chengyu.eu/images/2025/06/09/7e8cd27e607c758355f38df59d63cac5.mp4"></video>

Overall, that part of the experience wasn't great. Also, if you don't specify what a character in the scene looks like, it defaults to generating what looks like a Western news reporter.

<video controls src="https://media.chengyu.eu/images/2025/06/09/cb65309e7a63d5390c7f4925126a4172.mp4"></video>

<video controls src="https://media.chengyu.eu/images/2025/06/09/AI.mp4"></video>

Hopefully future versions improve on this — longer clips, and better Chinese-language support.
