Comparing Gemini and OpenAI: Gemini will happily generate several thousand words of text without holding back, while OpenAI is noticeably stingier by comparison. Still, ChatGPT's image generation is strong enough that I can't give it up — hand it a blurry photo of a painting on a wall, and it can read what's in it and recreate a new piece on the same theme.

It recreated the style of a Chinese woodblock print, along with what the original piece was trying to express.

![Recreating the woodblock-print style](https://media.chengyu.eu/images/2025/06/09/Pasted-image-20250522213837.jpg)

And it's genuinely fun to play with — the same subject can just as easily be switched over to a 3D clay-style rendering.

![The same subject rendered in a 3D clay style](https://media.chengyu.eu/images/2025/06/09/Pasted-image-20250522213855.jpg)
