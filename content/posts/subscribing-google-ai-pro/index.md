Starting today, I'm a Google AI Pro subscriber.

Right after Google's 2025 conference wrapped up, it felt like they'd rebuilt basically every one of their own products around AI.

With Google stepping up like that, honestly, it threw me for a loop. I used to be a committed OpenAI user. Now that both sides are pushing this hard, with each new feature more capable than the last, I'm genuinely torn on which one to actually use.

![Google's newly announced AI features](https://media.chengyu.eu/images/2025/06/09/Pasted-image-20250522212846.jpg)

The catch is that Google's newest features right now need their top-tier subscription — even at half price during the current promotion, the monthly fee still stings a bit.

For now I'm starting with the Pro tier. Things move fast enough in this space that some of those flashier features will probably trickle down to Pro before too long anyway.
